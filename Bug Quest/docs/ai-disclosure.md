All AI-generated material must be reviewed, understood, validated, and explainable to judges. Keep sensitive data out of public AI tools. When AI helps analyze an Automation Quest failure, record the source test/output reference, human reproduction, final classification, and evidence reviewed; AI analysis must not promote an unverified failure into a product defect. Keep sanitized working evidence in `evidence/` and only approved final material in `output/`.
